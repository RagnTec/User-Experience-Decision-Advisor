# Research brief

Use this as a working outline, not a mandatory form. Replace guidance with the current project's facts. Keep unknown consequential inputs marked “to confirm.”

## Decision and context

- **Decision to be made:** [What can the accountable owner choose, and by when?]
- **Business context:** [Why this decision matters now; product/version; reversibility.]
- **Users and tasks:** [Who performs which task, in what environment?]
- **Observed phenomenon:** [What users report or data show; source and date.]
- **Not yet established:** [Technical cause, mechanism, prevalence, or preference that remains a hypothesis.]

## Evidence and question

- **Existing evidence:** [Source, coverage, denominator if relevant, and what it can/cannot answer.]
- **Decision-critical unknown:** [The smallest unknown that could change the choice.]
- **Research question:** [Population/context + phenomenon + comparison or condition.]
- **Competing explanations:** [At least the strongest credible alternative.]

## Proposed approach

- **Preferred method and reason:** [Why this method can answer the question.]
- **Serious alternative:** [Lower-cost or stronger design, and why not selected.]
- **What the method cannot establish:** [Explicit inference boundary.]
- **Feasible scope:** [Time, people, recruitment lead time, prototype/data access, budget.]
- **Fallback:** [What can be done if a dependency fails, and how the conclusion narrows.]

## Deliverable and decision link

- **Output and audience:** [Brief, plan, instrument, analysis, findings, decision memo.]
- **Expected evidence pattern → decision:** [What result would support, weaken, or leave the choice open.]
- **Authorization needed:** [Recruitment, private data access, recording, publication, implementation.]
- **Owner and next checkpoint:** [Named role/person; date or condition.]

## Review before use

- Is the decision distinct from the desired conclusion?
- Are facts, hypotheses, and proposals separated?
- Can the proposed method answer the question within resources?
- Are consent, privacy, and data permissions explicit?
- Is there a stop or reversal condition?
