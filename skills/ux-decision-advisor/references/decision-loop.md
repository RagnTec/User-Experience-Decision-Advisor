# Decision-centred research loop | v0.4B

The unit of work is the **decision question**, not a mandatory research project. Do not generate a full study merely because a study method is available.

## Binding constraints before optional research

Apply [Jurisdiction & Norms Gate](jurisdiction-and-norms-gate.md) as an early risk-proportionate preflight for each novel change. Escalation/hold is applied to implementation/compliance claims where binding law is unclear; it is not a reason to fabricate certainty or block bounded UX discovery. Proposed industry-convention deviations require a named human decision, not implied authorization.

## Stages (not a mandatory linear wizard)
1. **Decision framing**: responsible audience, concrete alternatives including status quo, decision deadline if supplied, user task and consumer actors, constraints, actual unknowns, initial conditional recommendation.
2. **O/E/D preassessment**: identify possible opportunity, mechanisms and trade-offs with evidence status. Deliver a usable preliminary answer before any optional research invitation.
3. **Decision-critical evidence gate**: would resolving one unknown plausibly change the choice? If not, stop evidence collection. If yes, propose a minimal validation option **plus** 'continue with current evidence' and free-input alternative. Do not imply a study is mandatory.
4. **V research plan by request**: select method by question, not by familiarity. Optional outputs: focused interview questions, task-test protocol, questionnaire items; avoid leading questions, consent/sensitive-data collection, unvalidated scales and invented quotas.
5. **Evidence return**: user may return survey tables, task logs, coded feedback, VOC, other artifacts. Check permission, schema, coverage, sampling, denominators, exclusion and duplicate rules before analysis. Preserve raw files locally and do not copy them to a public repository. If tools unavailable, disclose what could not be computed.
6. **Evidence synthesis**: quantitative results with reproducible denominators and scripts; qualitative thematic codes with traceable excerpts and separation of mention counts from unique speakers; competing explanations and bias.
7. **Decision update**: link new evidence to original hypothesis/choice, mark UPHOLDS/QUALIFIES/CHANGES/DOES_NOT_RESOLVE, explain impact on alternatives, remaining uncertainty and affordable next action. A 'no change' decision is valuable. Respect research independence and do not fabricate gain or revenue.

## Targeted collaboration checkpoints
Ask only when a different answer changes the method, interpretation or deliverable. Available-data invitation (interview/VOC/de-identified survey/product process/engineering rules) is optional; user can skip. Present tentative result and why the requested input matters. At most 1-2 meaningful checkpoints in a typical project; users can direct 'finish now'. Existing context persists across turns.

## Decision and method constraints
- Existing user research is optional to START analysis; it is necessary, or equivalent appropriate evidence is necessary, to VERIFY actual preference distributions or outcomes.
- Market/consumer segments and actor roles must be grounded, not assumed from 'East/West'. Only compare like instruments, samples and constructs.
- `focused-experience-validation` provides optional V and data-return operations. It cannot recruit users, perform unattended studies, or infer causal effects from convenience/synthetic data.
- Do not auto-route all D/E/O outputs through V. Trigger V only if uncertainty can affect a consequential choice and user wants it.
- The quantitative script produces descriptive sample summaries; any causal or population generalization requires additional study design and assumptions checked by a professional.
- Treat reports from real projects as private, including H5, Decision Record, data logs and source quotes.

## External review and optional VOC
Invite VOC only when it can resolve a decision-relevant unknown; require source/provenance context before interpreting frequencies. If a user requests external-Skill comparison, use independently sourced methods, fixed comparator context and claim-level discrepancy adjudication, not output agreement as an accuracy proxy. See [VOC Source Routing](voc-source-routing.md) and [External Output Benchmark](external-output-benchmark.md).
