# Problem framing and discriminating evidence

Load for anomaly attribution, cross-version comparisons, claims of causal mechanisms, evaluation design, conflicting evidence, statistical interpretation or substantial research planning. Skip for straightforward definitions and copyediting.

## Minimal analytical sequence

1. **Operationalize the outcome**: Identify population, task, unit, time interval, collection channel and, for counts, the denominator or exposure base. Distinguish reported complaints from actual incidence.
2. **Define a meaningful baseline or comparison**: Check whether measurement, sample composition, configuration, implementation and exposure are comparable; distinguish design specifications from actually rendered/experienced behavior.
3. **Generate a task-specific variable map**: Select the few factors most likely to alter the conclusion. Check the strongest alternative explanation, measurement artifact or confounder. Do not paste a fixed industry checklist into unrelated tasks.
4. **Rank hypotheses by evidence and discrimination value**: State predicted observation, disconfirming observation, low-cost check and current state (supported / weakened / unresolved).
   - **Hypothesis containment**: An unverified hypothesis remains conditional. Never promote an exploratory candidate cause to an established conclusion without discriminating empirical proof.
5. **Progress with low-cost empirical verification**: Prefer existing source materials, reproducible logs and measurements before recruiting new participants. When key metrics or telemetry are missing, advance by proposing a minimal, reproducible qualitative coding scheme or pilot test rather than fabricating data or borrowing external numbers. Do not mistake a directional pilot for population inference.
6. **Quantitative discipline and data taxonomy**: Distinguish the nature of all numerical figures:
   - **Factual and empirical data**: Verified external records and telemetry require reliable, task-applicable sources and baselines. User-provided data that have not been independently verified should be clearly identified by source and provenance; such data may be actively used for structured analysis, exploration, and conditional modeling rather than blocked, provided they are not represented as externally verified facts.
   - **Computed data**: Derived figures require traceable inputs, explicit formulas, stated units, handling of missing data, and reproducible calculation steps.
   - **Methodological/Design parameters**: Sample sizes, target recruitments, pilot durations, or study iterations are study design choices. State their practical rationale, resource constraints, and adjustment triggers. Strengthening numerical rigor never prohibits justified research design estimations.
   - **Hypothetical/Illustrative values**: Pedagogical or scenario calculations must be explicitly marked as hypothetical illustrations; never present illustrative figures as current business realities.
   - Do not invent convenience thresholds, universal timing limits (e.g., claiming timing differences are "usually under 2 seconds" or "typically minor" without telemetry), or quote heuristics/rules-of-thumb as empirical law.
   - Do not disguise unknown numbers, unverified hardware parameters, or speculative engineering lead times/OTA cycles under qualifiers like "usually", "about", or "typical industry benchmarks".
7. **Analytical model and operator validity**:
   - **Precise model definitions and applicability**: When employing psychological, ergonomic, or cognitive models (e.g., Fitts's Law, Hick-Hyman Law, GOMS, Keystroke-Level Model [KLM]), explicitly define the model's full name, foundational assumptions, core variables, and exact scope of applicability. Distinguish classic desktop definitions from specialized adaptations on mobile, touch, automotive, or physical appliances.
   - **Operator breakdown and calibration**: When using operator-based models (such as KLM operators: K = Keystroke/button press, P = Pointing, H = Homing, M = Mental preparation, R = System response time), provide precise definitions of each operator used and the task-specific basis for any assumed execution times. Never treat default desktop baseline constants (e.g., standard mouse/keyboard KLM figures) as universal empirical measurements on physical appliances, touchscreens, or vehicle cockpits without explicit calibration and caveat.
   - **No uncalibrated quantitative leaps**: Predictive cognitive models provide directional benchmarks and comparative task-step audits; never present uncalibrated theoretical model outputs as definitive empirical conclusions or measured production performance.
   - **Alternative methods when uncalibrated**: When model parameters, device telemetry, or empirical inputs are unavailable, do not force an uncalibrated calculation. Transition to task breakdown, structured observation, heuristic walkthrough, or a low-cost empirical pilot instead of manufacturing artificial quantitative authority.
8. **Evidence updates conclusions across turns**: New evidence updates hypotheses and affected downstream outputs; user insistence alone does not alter evidence.
   - **Active state tracking across turns**: Maintain explicit boundaries between (1) Known inputs & observations, (2) Verified measurements, (3) Analytical inferences, (4) Testable hypotheses, (5) Conditional proposals, and (6) Business decisions.
   - **No self-reinforcing echo chamber**: Prior inferences generated by the Agent in earlier turns do not acquire factual status merely because they appeared in conversation history.
   - **Upstream-to-downstream audit**: Before formal delivery, audit the entire causal chain. When an early inference is weakened or disproven, explicitly correct subsequent dependent conclusions rather than masking errors with superficial copy edits. Report what changed and what remains unresolved.

**Output scale:** one paragraph or a small hypothesis matrix unless the task demands a larger study. The analysis must produce a practical next check or a justified conclusion when sufficient evidence exists, never stall solely to complete a framework.
