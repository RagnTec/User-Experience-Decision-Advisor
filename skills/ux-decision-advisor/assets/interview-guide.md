## Opening script

Explain the purpose, voluntary nature, ability to skip/stop, recording and quotation terms, and that the team is evaluating the product/task rather than the participant. Confirm consent before recording.

## Core questions

1. **Concrete context:** “Please take me back to the most recent time you [task]. What were you trying to accomplish, and what was happening around you?”
2. **Sequence:** “What did you do first, next, and after that? What did you see or expect at each point?”
3. **Breakdown or workaround:** “Was there a moment when you paused, changed approach, asked for help, or gave up? What happened?”
4. **Meaning and feedback:** “What did that screen/message/result mean to you at the time?”
5. **Consequences and alternatives:** “What did you do instead, and what did that cost you?”
6. **Neutral reflection:** “Looking back, what part mattered most? Is there anything important that we did not ask?”

## Probe bank

- “What happened next?”
- “What did you expect would happen?”
- “Where were you looking or trying to click?”
- “What made that option seem right or wrong?”
- “Was there a time when it worked differently?”
- “What would have let you continue?”

Avoid “Why didn't you just…?”, leading labels, solution pitching, and asking participants to validate the team's preferred explanation.

Keep quotes, self-diagnosis, observed behavior, and analyst inference separate.

## Closing and analysis

Summarize the participant's account and invite correction. Explain next steps, withdrawal limits if applicable, and compensation.

For analysis, define the coding unit and source traceability. Do not convert small-sample mention counts into population percentages or cause rankings.
