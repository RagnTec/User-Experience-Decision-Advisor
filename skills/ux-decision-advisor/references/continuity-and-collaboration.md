# Continuous research co-creation and handoff

Use for multi-turn work, revised constraints, supplied artifacts, or formal project handoff.

## 1. Preserve user ownership

Start from the user's actual decision, materials, and proposed approach. Do not turn a questionnaire review into a new competitor study or a design review into an unauthorized implementation plan.

Mirror the scope accurately enough to show understanding. Mark a consequential missing input as `待确认` or “to confirm”; do not fill it with a guess. Proceed conditionally when the missing detail does not block useful analysis.

## 2. Incremental update pattern

When new information arrives:

1. restate only the affected decision or constraint;
2. state which prior judgments remain valid;
3. identify which conclusions are qualified, changed, or unresolved;
4. revise the affected artifact or section;
5. preserve still-valid rationale and user wording;
6. update resources, decision conditions, and downstream dependencies.

Do not regenerate the entire plan for a local change. Do not let an earlier assistant inference acquire factual status merely because it appears in history.

## 3. Changed constraints are not new evidence

If time, budget, recruitment, or prototype access changes:

- reassess feasibility first;
- downgrade the method or sample only with an explicit rationale;
- narrow the claim accordingly;
- retain still-valid research questions and hypotheses;
- identify which decision now remains open.

A narrower feasible study is better than a complete-looking study that cannot run.

## 4. Artifact checkpoints

Use checkpoints only when they help the user:

| Checkpoint | Artifact | Review focus |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | research brief | decision, questions, evidence, constraints |
| Design | protocol | validity, users, sample, materials, analysis, ethics, feasibility |
| Prepare | instruments/data dictionary | wording, routing, tasks, event semantics, permissions |
| Analyze | reproducible notes/code | units, cleaning, denominators, coding, uncertainty |
| Report | findings and decision brief | result → interpretation → consequence → limits |
| Update | decision/next-study note | what changed, owner, stop or reversal condition |

A checkpoint is not authorization to collect data, publish, or implement.

## 5. Reviewing supplied artifacts

For a supplied plan, questionnaire, dataset, or result:

- identify what is already sound;
- locate the concrete defect and its consequence;
- propose the smallest targeted revision;
- show before/after when wording matters;
- check downstream consistency;
- keep unsupported fields unknown rather than inventing them.

## 6. Multi-turn consistency audit

Before formal delivery, check:

- research questions still match the business decision;
- hypotheses still connect to methods and measures;
- sample and recruitment still fit the current resources;
- tasks and conditions still support the intended comparison;
- metrics and analysis units have not drifted;
- evidence labels remain accurate;
- limitations and reversal conditions reflect the latest evidence;
- no proposal is presented as an approved decision.

## 7. Handoff note

A concise handoff should include:

- current decision and owner;
- baseline/version and current artifact locations;
- known facts, hypotheses, and evidence gaps;
- current recommendation and reversal condition;
- executed versus planned work;
- data/source permissions and restrictions;
- unresolved risks and next feasible action.

Keep raw data and private materials outside public skill sources.
