# Product-domain Teaching Map | v0.5C — contextual extensions, not hard-coded rules

The consumer product shapes *tasks, usage environment, roles, incentives and constraints*. Begin from product facts supplied by the user. **Nothing in this table certifies current market conditions, behavior rates, engineering requirements, law, or standards**. If product type is unknown, mark unknown, keep common research teaching valid and request only information critical to the next decision.

| Product/experience | Domain teaching prompts | Useful baseline research and optional evidence | What not to infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automotive cockpit / companion app | Driver vs passenger vs remote operator; parked vs moving; state visibility, divided attention, key sharing and failure recovery | Task-state map, context-aware task walkthrough, failure-mode interview, optional controlled usability study; safety specialist assesses engineering risks | No universal distance/time, brake/interlock or legal clause from UX intuition; remote cabin conditioning != drive-ready state |
| Smartphone / wearable / ecosystem | Device handoff, portability, one-handed use, multimodal input, permissions, battery/network constraints | Longitudinal diary, task observation, notification comprehension, accessibility check | Do not assume OS, sensor ability or cross-platform equivalence |
| Ecommerce / local consumer services | Consideration, payment, fulfillment, returns and customer support; buyer/payer/recipient roles, trust and price | Funnel definitions, checkout task test, VOC with dedup and source bias, journey mapping with real touchpoints | Conversion change alone cannot diagnose mental cause; platform/merchant samples are not whole market |
| Media / social / content / games | Exposure, engagement, creator/platform incentives, moderation, recommendation, user motives and social effects | Discovery interviews, comprehension task, feed/content usage diary, properly instrumented exposure/engagement analysis | Views/likes/retention are not equivalent to satisfaction, autonomy or persuasion |
| Household IoT / appliance | Multi-person roles, shared spaces, installation/maintenance, environmental context, accessibility | In-home contextual inquiry, task analysis, setup tests, error-log review | Device telemetry alone cannot attribute motivation; no universal human measurement cutoff |
| Finance/health-related/wellness consumer UX | High trust and comprehension stakes, consequences of error, privacy, vulnerability and regulated marketing/communication | Comprehension checks, accessible communication tests, contextual task analysis, qualified legal/domain review | Do not provide medical/financial professional certification or assert local regulatory compliance without verified scope |
| Children / older adults / family shared products | Consent and proxy roles, abilities, caregiver mediation, household dynamics, developmental/accessibility variation | Role and stakeholder map, age-appropriate supervised observation and protocol, ethical/privacy screening | Avoid age-essentialism; require actual consent and safeguards; do not infer a family preference from a single speaker |

### Transfer rule: mechanism match before importing a pattern

Use `(1) source product task/context → (2) target product task/context → (3) shared human mechanism → (4) key differences / safety / norms → (5) candidate evidence and possible disconfirmation`. Similar UI appearance alone does not justify moving a pattern across products. If a safety-sensitive domain appears, distinguish UX risk hypothesis from engineering functional safety case and applicable official standards; report specialist validation as pending if not actually performed.

### How to choose how much domain detail to teach

- **Entry learner**: a concrete everyday example, one domain-specific difference, one practical question; don't assume they know acronyms.
- **Practitioner in another domain**: show transfer validity/breakpoints, relevant measurement and alternative interpretations.
- **Vertical UXR specialist**: discuss complex boundary cases, empirical validity and dependent decisions, cite verified domain sources when available.
- These are *user-selected depth strategies*, not labels assigned to people. The Agent can switch depth within the same project and preserve evidence and approvals.
